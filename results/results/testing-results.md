# Testing Results

## 1. Testing Objective

The bicycle prototype was evaluated to check its:

- Maneuverability
- Stability
- Rider control
- Braking response
- Comfort
- Practical usability

## 2. Maneuverability Testing

The bicycle was tested for practical maneuverability during the project evaluation.

The testing focused on:

- Steering control
- Turning ability
- Rider response
- Stability during movement

## 3. Comfort Evaluation

The spring-based suspension below the seat was evaluated for its ability to reduce shocks and vibrations during riding.

The design was intended to improve comfort on uneven road surfaces.

## 4. Braking Evaluation

The mechanical braking system was checked for:

- Braking response
- Rider control
- Stopping reliability
- Ease of operation

## 5. Prototype Evaluation

The complete bicycle was evaluated after integrating:

- Z-frame
- Suspension
- Handlebar
- Mechanical brakes
- Platform pedals
- Wheels and tyres
- Kickstand

## 6. Competition Result

The prototype participated in the **SAE Bicycle Design Challenge 2025–26** as **Team Pegasus (BDC2025020)**.

### Achievement

**Maneuverability Award – ₹5,000 Cash Prize**

## 7. Final Result

The developed bicycle demonstrated the intended focus on:

- Practical urban use
- Rider comfort
- Stability
- Maneuverability
- Sustainable design
