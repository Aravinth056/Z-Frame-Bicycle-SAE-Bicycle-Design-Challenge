# Components

## Frame

- Z-frame structure
- CR (Cold Rolled) frame material
- Designed for strength, durability and compactness

## Suspension

- Single spring suspension
- Located below the seat
- Provides shock absorption and improved riding comfort

## Braking System

- Standard mechanical brakes
- Designed for safe and reliable stopping
- Low-maintenance arrangement

## Handlebar

- Ergonomic design
- Enhanced grip
- Supports comfortable riding posture and control

## Pedals

- Platform-type pedals
- Wide and stable foot placement
- Slip-resistant design

## Wheels and Tyres

- Standard 26-inch wheels
- Urban tyres
- Designed for reliable road contact and traction

## Parking Support

- Side-mounted kickstand
- Provides stable and convenient parking
